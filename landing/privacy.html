Privacy Policy

Privacy by Default & GDPR Compliance Statement

Privacy by Default

OnlyWinget is built on the philosophy that you shouldn't have to sacrifice your privacy to get a convenient user interface. The app is entirely local. This means there are no intermediary servers, cloud tracking, or centralized databases monitoring your actions.

GDPR Compliance & Your Rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), European users have specific data protection rights. Because OnlyWinget operates 100% locally on your computer, your rights are fully respected by design:

  • Data Minimization: We do not collect, process, or transmit any personal data. You are completely anonymous.
  • Right to Access: Your settings, packages lists, and workspace configurations are stored as human-readable JSON files in local appdata. You have complete visibility and ownership of your data at all times.
  • Right to Erasure (Right to be Forgotten): Uninstalling the application or deleting the workspace file removes 100% of the application state from your computer. No data persists on any remote server.
  • Data Portability: You can export your app configurations (Presets) easily using a plain JSON text format to move them to any other device.

External Data Connections

The application communicates directly with official Microsoft services to perform its tasks. No traffic is routed through third-party platforms. OnlyWinget connects solely to:

  • Microsoft Winget Repository: To download and install packages.
  • Windows Update Service: Via local Windows Update COM interface to scan and download operating system updates.

No Cookies or Trackers

This website and the application do not use cookies, tracking pixels, or third-party analytical tools. We do not monitor how long you stay on the landing page or which features you use inside the app.
