Local storage and network connections

About this notice

This page describes what the OnlyWinget desktop app stores on your PC and which external services the app and this website use.

Data stored on your PC

By default, OnlyWinget stores its application data in %LOCALAPPDATA%\OnlyWinget.

  • Workspace: Presets, their package lists and your active preset are stored in the onlywinget.db SQLite database.
  • Settings and diagnostics: Settings and source preferences are stored in JSON files. Diagnostic logs are stored in the logs folder.
  • Uninstall: The OnlyWinget uninstaller preserves the application data folder, including the database, settings and logs.
  • Export: You can export individual presets as JSON files.

Desktop app connections

OnlyWinget delegates package and operating system operations to WinGet and Windows Update.

  • WinGet: WinGet contacts the selected, enabled sources, which may include custom sources you configure. Package downloads may come from addresses specified by those sources.
  • Windows Update: Windows handles scans and downloads through its configured update service.

Website connections

The home and privacy pages load fonts from Google Fonts and icons from Cloudflare’s cdnjs. Your browser connects to those providers and shares network information such as your IP address in the course of those requests.
